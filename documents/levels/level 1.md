# Level 1 Technical Specification

**Level Identity & Pacing**

* **Total Distance:** $600\text{m}$ ($0\text{m} - 500\text{m}$ scripted waves $\rightarrow 500\text{m} - 600\text{m}$ boss arena).
* **Coordinate System:** Normalized horizontal axis from $X = -1.0$ (Far Left) to $X = +1.0$ (Far Right), where $X = 0.0$ is river center.
* **XP Progression Target:**
* Wave 1 yields $\ge 100\text{ XP}$ to unlock **Tier 2 (Monkey Bomber)** right before the first hazard squeeze.


* **Scroll Speed:** $12.0\text{ m/s}$ (normal cruising), dropping to $6.0\text{ m/s}$ during the boss encounter.

**Entity Roster**

* **Coin Arc:** Clustered doubloons tracing smooth racing lines.
* **Wooden Barrel ($1\text{ HP}$):** $+15\text{ XP}$, $+10$ Gold.
* **Armored Barrel ($2\text{ HP}$):** $+25\text{ XP}$, drops Golden Chest ($+50\text{ XP}$).
* **Full-Width Barricade:** 5 interlocking wood segments ($2\text{ HP}$ each). Must shoot 1 segment to break through.
* **Raider Skiff ($3\text{ HP}$):** Fast-moving mob moving down-river.
* **Patrol Boat ($6\text{ HP}$):** Heavy armored boat.
* **Spiked Sea Mine:** Detonates instantly upon boat collision.
* **Boss: Captain Ironjaw’s War-Barge ($60\text{ HP}$):**
* Destructible side swivel guns ($5\text{ HP}$ each).
* Armor plating ($10\text{ HP}$ each).
* Exposed Boiler Core ($40\text{ HP}$) taking critical direct cannon damage during steam vents.
